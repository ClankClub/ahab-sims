Use when the owner gives you a date, deadline or event more than 30 days away, or asks you to remember something for months ahead — alarms reach 30 days at most, and repeat only does daily/weekdays/weekly.

The pattern: one self-renewing monthly sweep alarm, whose message is fixed and points at a list in MEMORY.md. The file changes; the alarm never does.

## Steps

1. Add the item to the "Long-dated diary" line in MEMORY.md: date, what it is, time if there is one. Keep it in date order so the sweep can read it at a glance.
2. If a sweep alarm already exists, don't touch it — the message reads MEMORY.md, so a new date needs no alarm change. This is the whole point and the thing I got wrong four times on 4-5 Jan.
3. If no sweep alarm exists (first time, or it has lapsed), set one about 29 days out. Message, verbatim:

   "Long-dated diary sweep (monthly, self-renewing). Read the 'Long-dated diary' line in MEMORY.md for the tracked items. Job: for each item now within 30 days, set 7-day and 1-day reminders. Then re-arm this sweep ~29 days ahead."

4. When the sweep fires: for each item now within 30 days, set real reminders (7-day and 1-day), then remove those items from the line and re-arm the sweep ~29 days ahead.
5. Corrections and extensions both edit in place. "I had it wrong" replaces the date everywhere (line, USER.md, the person's page). Someone else moving a date (Tomas pushing a book, Wilhelm pushing a return) is an extension: update the line, and keep the old date on the person's page as history.
6. Clashes get named, not resolved. When a new date lands on top of something already tracked (the dentist on 15 Sep, inside the Valparaíso trip, and Noor's birthday on the last day), say so in one line and leave both on the line. She decides; I hold.

## Pitfalls

- **Insert by date, not by when you heard it.** On 8 Jan, Yvette's 25 October went in after Marlene's 24 September but ahead of a physio on 11 October, so the line read out of order and needed a second edit a minute later. Read the dates either side of the insertion point before writing.
- Reordering the whole line to "tidy" it. Don't; the line's stability is what makes small edits safe.
- Putting the detail in the alarm message instead of in MEMORY.md. The message is fixed and points at the file, deliberately — a message that carries the list is a message I have to rebuild every time.

## How you know it worked

- The new item is on the line, in date order, with its date and time.
- No new alarm was created, or exactly one if there wasn't one.
- Nothing already on the line was lost or reworded.
