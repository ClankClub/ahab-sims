# Stretching Routine

*Use this skill when the owner requests a quick, physical routine for neck or shoulder tension.*

## Steps
1. **Neck Tilts:** Slowly tilt your head to the right, trying to bring your ear toward your shoulder. Hold for 15-20 seconds. Repeat on the left side.
2. **Shoulder Rolls:** Gently roll your shoulders up towards your ears, back, and down. Do this 10 times in each direction.
3. **Chin Tucks:** Sit or stand tall. Gently pull your chin straight back as if making a double chin, keeping your eyes level. Hold for 5 seconds. Repeat 10 times.
4. **Gentle Side Bends:** Slowly drop your left ear toward your left shoulder, stretching the right side of your neck. Hold for 15 seconds. Repeat on the other side.
5. **Shoulder Stretches:** Clasp your hands behind your back and gently lift your arms up to stretch the upper back and shoulders. Hold for 30 seconds.

## Pitfalls
- Never stretch to the point of pain. If the owner reports pain, stop immediately.

## How to know it worked
- The owner reports reduced neck tension and comfort.
