# Five-minute neck and shoulder routine

For ordinary desk-tension neck. Move slowly, breathe, stay well short of pain.

1. **Settle (30s).** Sit or stand tall, shoulders down and back. Four slow breaths, jaw loose.
2. **Chin tucks (60s).** Pull chin straight back like making a double chin — not down. Hold 3s. 8 slow reps.
3. **Slow turns (45s).** Look right until you feel a gentle stretch, hold 5s, back through centre to the left. Nothing forced.
4. **Ear to shoulder (45s).** Drop right ear toward right shoulder, hold 20s, let the arm hang heavy — don't pull it down with your hand. Swap sides.
5. **Shoulder blade squeezes (40s).** Draw shoulder blades back and down, hold 3s. 8 reps.
6. **Doorway chest stretch (60s).** Forearms on a door frame, step through gently, 30s each side. This is the one that fixes the neck long-term.
7. **Shake out (20s).** Shoulders roll, arms loose.

## Good to know

- Do it twice a day if the neck is grumpy — morning and mid-afternoon beat one long session.
- Skip full neck circles; they grind the joints for no gain.
- Tingling or numbness down an arm, weakness, a sudden severe headache, dizziness, or neck pain after a knock: see a person, not a list.
