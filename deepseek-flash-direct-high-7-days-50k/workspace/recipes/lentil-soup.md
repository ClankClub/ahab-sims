# Red lentil soup, about 40 minutes

Serves 4. Red lentils need no soaking and collapse into the soup, which is why this is fast.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil (or butter)
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped small
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp paprika (smoked if you have it), pinch of chilli
- 250g red lentils, rinsed until the water runs clear
- 1 litre stock (veg or chicken) + 250ml water
- Juice of half a lemon — do not skip
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Oil in a wide pot, medium heat. Onion + carrot, 8 minutes until soft and starting to colour.
2. Garlic, 1 minute. Tomato paste and spices, 1 minute — fry them until they smell nutty, this is where the flavour comes from.
3. Lentils + stock + water. Boil, then simmer 20-25 minutes until the lentils have fallen apart. Stir now and then so it doesn't catch.
4. Salt and pepper. Blend half of it if you want it creamy but still with texture, or all of it for smooth.
5. Lemon juice off the heat, then taste and adjust salt. The lemon is what makes it taste finished rather than flat.

## Levers
- Thicker: less water, or simmer 10 minutes longer.
- Faster: pressure cooker, 8 minutes at pressure after step 2.
- More substance: a handful of rice or small pasta in with the lentils.
- Topping: fried cumin in oil poured over at the end; or yoghurt and mint.
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge, freezes well.
