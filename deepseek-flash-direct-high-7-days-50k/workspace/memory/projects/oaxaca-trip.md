## Easter collision (CORRECTED 2027-01-08)

- Easter Sunday 2027 is **28 March**, not 27 March. I told the owner 27 March on 5 Jan — that was wrong, and I've corrected it with them. Computed twice with the standard Gregorian computus (a=13, b=3, c=4, k=20, p=6, q=5, M=24, N=5, d=1, e=5 → 22+1+5 = 28 March) and matches the known sequence 2025 Apr 20 / 2026 Apr 5 / 2027 Mar 28. Still not confirmed against an outside source, because the web tools are broken.
- Consequences, which are worse than what I first told them: Good Friday is 26 March — the day they plan to arrive. Holy Thursday is 25 March. Easter Sunday 28 March falls inside the stay. Oaxaca's Semana Santa is one of the biggest in Mexico; the Procession of Silence is on Good Friday. So arriving on the 26th means landing into the peak of it, with closures, crowds and inflated prices.
- Options: shift to 2-6 April for a calm city, or go 20-25 March to be there before the crush, or keep 26-30 March and embrace Semana Santa deliberately (book everything now, expect it to be busy and expensive).
- Prep worth doing later: flights 2-3 months ahead for March, accommodation near the centre, afternoons still hot.
