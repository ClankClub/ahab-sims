# Noodle — my owner's dog

- Name: Noodle. Female ("she").
- Allergic to soy. Told me on 2027-01-04.
- Practical consequence: soy hides in a lot of dog food, treats and chews (soybean meal, soy protein, hydrolysed soy, and "vegetable" ingredients that mean soy). Check labels before recommending anything edible; when I suggest treats or food, lead with soy-free options.
