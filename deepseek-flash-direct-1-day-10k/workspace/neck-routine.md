# Five-minute neck routine

For a stiff, achy neck from sitting. Twice a day: once in the morning, once mid-afternoon. Warm shower first helps. Slow and gentle throughout — no forcing, no pain, nothing that makes you dizzy.

**1. Chin tucks (1 min).** Sit tall. Draw your chin straight back — you're making a double chin, not nodding down. Hold 3 seconds, release. 10 slow reps. This one is the actual fix for desk posture, so don't skip it.

**2. Turn and look (1 min).** Turn your head slowly to the left as far as comfortable, hold 5 seconds, come back through centre, then right, hold 5 seconds. About 3 each side.

**3. Ear to shoulder (1 min).** Tip your head so the left ear heads toward the left shoulder. Let the weight of your head do the work — don't pull with your hand. 20–30 seconds, breathe, then the other side.

**4. Look into your armpit (1 min).** Turn your head 45 degrees to the left, look down toward your armpit, and give a small nod. You'll feel it at the back of the neck on that side. 20–30 seconds each side.

**5. Shoulders (1 min).** Three slow shrugs — up for 5 seconds, drop for 5. Then 10 shoulder rolls backwards. Then stand in a doorway with forearms on the frame and lean through it for 30 seconds to open the chest; tight chest pulls the head forward.

## Worth knowing

- The stretch feels good for about twenty minutes. The chin tucks and the doorway stretch are what change anything long-term.
- Check your screen height: top of the monitor at eye level. And if you wake up sore, suspect the pillow before you suspect the stretches.
- Stop and see someone if you get numbness or tingling into the arm or hand, weakness, dizziness, a severe headache, or if this started with a fall or a jolt. Those aren't stretching problems.
