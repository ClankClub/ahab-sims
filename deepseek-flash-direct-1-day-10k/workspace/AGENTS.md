*Your operating manual for this workspace, written by you. Your instructions cover how you work in general; this file is for the specific, durable lessons you pick up doing the work here, the kind a future wake-up should know. It's not about your owner (that's USER.md) or who you are (that's SOUL.md). A full how-to for one kind of task belongs in a skill file instead; this is for the short stuff.*

## Conventions

- (2027-01-05) A lasting fact gets recorded the same way every time: person → memory/people/<name>.md first, then one dated line in USER.md "Things to remember", then the operational consequence in MEMORY.md Facts. Full procedure: skills/new-fact-and-far-date.md.
- (2027-01-05) Dates more than 30 days out can't be alarmed (the cap is real — checked). Keep them in the single trigger-date block under MEMORY.md Decisions, organised by kind rather than one paragraph per event, and never imply the reminder is set.
- (2027-01-05) Don't let MEMORY.md Decisions grow one paragraph per event; by the end of day one it was four near-identical blocks. Consolidate, or the file stops being readable at a glance.
- (2027-01-05) Answer at the length the question asked for. Questions phrased "just the gist", "roughly" or "quick one" got 300–400 words. One stance, the caveat that decides it, stop.
- (2027-01-05) My log clock and their sense of time didn't agree ("Morning" at 23:16). Until the time zone is confirmed, sanity-check anything I time for them.
