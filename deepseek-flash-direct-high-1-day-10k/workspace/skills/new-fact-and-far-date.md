# Recording a lasting fact or a far-off date

Use when your owner tells you a fact about themselves, a person, or a date or obligation that has to survive past this wake-up — "my sister's birthday is…", "I borrowed X from Y, due…", "I'm going to Z in March".

## Steps

1. Sort what it is before writing:
   - a person → memory/people/<name>.md, first line saying who they are to your owner, then dated bullets
   - a date or obligation → one dated line in MEMORY.md Facts, and in USER.md "Things to remember" if it's personal
   - a stated preference about contact → PROACTIVE_PREFERENCES.md, in their words, never paraphrased
2. Write the person page first if a person is involved: it's where later detail will land, and saying "they've got a page now" tells your owner why telling you more is worth it.
3. Add the dated line(s). Don't paste the whole page into MEMORY.md — the fact plus a pointer is enough.
4. Check the date against today, and be straight about which case it is:
   - within 30 days → set real alarms: a week ahead and the day before for a deadline; two days ahead plus the morning of for an appointment
   - more than 30 days → add it to the trigger-date block under MEMORY.md Decisions and say plainly that the reminder depends on me still running, and their own calendar should hold the real one
5. In the reply: confirm it back in three words, add the one thing they don't know (soy hides in "natural" dog chews; flights are cheapest 6–10 weeks out; *Middlemarch* has a slow middle), and don't re-ask what they just told you.

## Pitfalls

- Saying "I'll remind you" about a date past 30 days. That's a promise the system can't keep.
- One MEMORY.md decision paragraph per event. By the end of the first day there were four near-identical blocks; consolidate into one block organised by kind.
- Recording a project name with no content and then writing as if you know the project. Say what's missing and ask one small question.
- Asking a clarifying question and not recording that the answer never came. Put the unanswered question in the line itself, and how many times you've asked.

## How you know it worked

On a later wake-up — or when they test you with "when's my physio appointment?" — the date, place and caveat come back without searching. 2027-01-05: four such checks in a row, all correct from notes.
